Future GTM Sprints

The MVP works on synthetic data. The next three sprints test whether real operational risk teams value it, in this order: is the challenge quality good, does it save time on real data, and will someone pay for it.

Sprint 1: Practitioner validation

1. Goal Learn whether the agent's flags and challenge questions match what experienced Second Line reviewers would raise themselves, and what is missing.

2. Target user / buyer Second Line operational risk / non-financial risk managers and analysts at banks and fintechs in Germany (BaFin-regulated, MaRisk and DORA scope).

3. Channel or motion Personal network from previous op risk roles plus direct LinkedIn outreach. 30-minute 1:1 walkthrough sessions (video call).

4. Key deliverable A live walkthrough on synthetic data, the two sample reports from samples/, and a short feedback form (rating per challenge: "I would raise this", "partly", "no").

5. Success metric At least 5 sessions held, and at least 3 of 5 reviewers rate the majority of challenges as "I would raise this". Top 3 missing features collected.

Sprint 2: Free pilot on real (anonymised) data

1. Goal Test whether the agent saves review time in a real RCSA cycle and whether its high-priority flags hold up against a human reviewer.

2. Target user / buyer Head of Operational Risk / NFR at a mid-sized fintech or small bank that runs an annual or semi-annual RCSA and has a small Second Line team.

3. Channel or motion Warm introductions from Sprint 1 participants, plus Berlin RegTech and risk management communities and meetups.

4. Key deliverable A 4-week pilot. The agent runs locally in the client's environment on an anonymised RCSA export, side by side with the normal manual review. No data leaves the client without approval; the LLM step can run with an approved provider or in --no-llm mode.

5. Success metric 1 pilot started. Review time per process reduced by at least 30% compared with the manual baseline, and at least 70% of high-priority flags confirmed as valid by the reviewer.

Sprint 3: Offer and channel test

1. Goal Test willingness to pay and whether consultancies are a better channel than selling directly to banks.

2. Target user / buyer GRC and operational risk consultancies that run RCSA cycles for clients, and GRC tool vendors looking for an AI challenge layer.

3. Channel or motion Partner outreach to consultancies, plus LinkedIn content: an anonymised sample report and a short post on "how the agent challenges an RCSA without scoring it".

4. Key deliverable A one-pager and a fixed-price offer: an "RCSA challenge review" per process, or a white-label licence for partners. Pilot results from Sprint 2 as the reference case.

5. Success metric 10 qualified conversations, and at least 2 letters of intent or paid engagements at the stated price.
